## Changes for travelers

| Feature | Integrated behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Costs | The selected display currency applies to debts and repayments. Each expense first uses its saved rate for the currency pair, then the trip rate, then the system rate. Confirming a repayment uses the displayed quote. Historical payments keep their recorded values. |
| Visit duration | Road Trip uses the upstream per-visit editor. The regular itinerary also edits that visit's duration. Both support zero through 10,080 minutes. The trip PDF retains the timing projection in the upstream layout. |
| Dashboard and sessions | The desktop and mobile dashboards use the upstream trip-date display. The exact departure countdown was removed. Remembered login on the fork's deployment remains 30 days. |
| Other fork features | Ordered common currencies, guest identity transfer, mobile day-selection recovery, Hong Kong Traditional Chinese, and the fork's Legal and Source information are carried forward. The fork keeps its Lightsail pipeline and optional Sonar check. |

For a two-person split of ¥5,000 at HK$1 = ¥50, the displayed debt is HK$50. A partial payment uses the same quote. When several people share expenses at different rates, switching currencies can change who owes whom after the balances are simplified. A confirmed payment clears its quoted amount in the selected currency. A different currency view can show a residual in that case.
