## The short version

The app is a client for a TREK server that you choose. Everything you enter in the app goes to that server and is stored there. We, the publisher of the app, receive none of it: no account, no analytics, no crash reports, no advertising identifiers, no tracking.

## Where your data goes

- **Your TREK server.** When you connect the app to a server, the app sends what you enter (your account details, trips, places, bookings, documents, photos, expenses, messages, journal entries and, if you allow it, your location for the map) to that server. Whoever runs that server is responsible for it: you, if you host it yourself, or the organisation that provides it. Their own privacy policy applies to it.
- **The public demo.** If you choose "Try the demo", the app connects to demo.liketrek.com, which we run. The demo is shared by everyone who tries it and is reset every hour; do not enter real personal data there.
- **Map tiles and exchange rates.** To draw maps, the app loads map tiles from the tile provider your server is configured with (by default OpenStreetMap-based providers such as OpenFreeMap). These requests carry the map area you look at and your IP address, as every web request does. Currency conversion rates come from a public exchange-rate service. These providers receive no account or trip data.
- **The system browser.** Signing in with single sign-on or a passkey opens the system browser on your server's sign-in page.

## Data on your device

The app keeps the server address, your session cookie and, for trips you choose to keep offline, a copy of those trips, their files and map tiles on your device. Removing the app removes all of it. On Android the app is excluded from device backups, so your session does not travel to other devices.

## Permissions

The camera, microphone and photo library are used only when you add a photo, a video or a document. Location is used only to centre the map on you. Each is asked for by the system when first needed and can be withdrawn in the system settings.

## Deleting your data

Delete your account in the app under Settings > Account > Delete account, or ask the administrator of your server. This removes the account and its data from that server. See https://liketrek.com/delete-account. Because we do not hold your data, we cannot delete it for you, but we will help you find the right contact.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly receive data from them.
