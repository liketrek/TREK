# TREK Travel Planner: Delete your account

_Draft. The publisher fills in the bracketed fields before this is published at https://liketrek.com/delete-account._

Your TREK account and everything in it live on the TREK server you signed in to, not with [PUBLISHER LEGAL NAME], the publisher of the app. You can delete it in either of these ways:

## In the app

1. Open the app and sign in.
2. Go to **Settings > Account**.
3. Choose **Delete account** and confirm.

The account is deleted on the server at once, together with its trips you own, your files, photos, journal entries and settings. Trips you share with others stay with their other members; your contributions to them are removed or anonymised.

## Without the app

Open your TREK server in a web browser, sign in and go to **Settings > Account > Delete account**. If you cannot sign in any more, ask the administrator of your server to delete the account.

## The public demo

Accounts on demo.liketrek.com cannot be deleted one by one: the demo is shared and is reset to its sample data every hour, which removes everything entered there.

## Data on your device

Removing the app from your phone or tablet removes the server address, your session and the trips kept offline.
